About Swoosh

Born from a lifelong passion for tennis and a drive to make professional-grade tracking accessible to every player.

The Story Behind Swoosh

Swoosh began as my final engineering project in college. As an avid tennis player, I was constantly frustrated by how difficult it was to record my matches to analyze my technique. Hiring a camera operator wasn't an option, and static tripods were essentially useless for a dynamic sport. That frustration sparked a vision: to build a smart, robotic mount that acts as a personal cameraman.

The Problem with Tripods

Today, the most common solution for amateur players is placing a static smartphone tripod at the edge of the court. This approach is flawed: a static camera frequently misses plays at the edges of the court, provides no automatic feedback on boundaries, and leaves players with hours of raw footage that is tedious to watch.

The Swoosh Solution

We realized the need for a smart, portable, and affordable tracking system. Swoosh combines an active IoT hardware mount with an intelligent mobile app. By tracking the ball in real-time and automatically panning the camera, the system ensures the action is always perfectly in frame, upgrading the training experience for anyone.

Powered by Advanced Tech

Swoosh bridges the physical and digital worlds. The robotic mount uses an ESP32 microcontroller and high-torque servo motors, communicating seamlessly via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). On the software side, a Flutter mobile app runs a custom YOLOv8 machine-learning model to detect the tennis ball in real-time at 30fps, while Firebase securely syncs your videos to the cloud.
