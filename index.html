The Smart Mount

Our intelligent IoT phone mount connects via Bluetooth Low Energy to physically pan and tilt your smartphone. Built with high-torque, lightning-fast servos and an ESP32 microcontroller, the mount reacts instantly to keep the action perfectly centered while you play.

The Swoosh App

The companion app serves as the brains of the operation. Running an advanced AI ball-tracking model in real-time right on your phone, it predicts the ball's trajectory, commands the mount where to look, and logs your session statistics.

System Specifications

Microcontroller ESP32 with ultra-low latency BLE 5.0
Actuators Dual-axis high-speed servo motors (Pan: 180°, Tilt: 90°)
Battery Life 10000mAh rechargeable lithium-ion (Up to 12 hours of continuous play)
AI Tracking Model Custom trained YOLOv8-nano, optimized for mobile NPU execution
App Compatibility iOS 14.0 or later / Android 10.0 or later
